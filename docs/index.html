AI Video Pipeline

An open-source tool that finds new AI tools, open-source GitHub projects and AI news from public sources, turns them into short vertical videos, and publishes them to the operator's own YouTube, Facebook and Instagram channels through the platforms' official APIs.

The app is operated by the owner of the channels it posts to. It is not a service for the general public and has no user accounts.

Source code: https://github.com/oceanfarm1992-design/Github-Video
